Intro – A Lunch for a Crowd

5 min
Ask the group “What would you pack for lunch if you were going out for the day?”
Ask “What if everyone in the room needed to eat and we had only one lunch? What could we do?”
Recap how Jesus calmed the storm. Tell the children that today they’ll see what Jesus did when a huge crowd needed something to eat.

Game – Magnetic Fishing

10 min

Setting Up

  • Print the A4 fish sheet in colour, cut out the fish and slide a steel paperclip onto the nose of each fish.
  • Scatter them on a blue mat or inside a taped circle. Set out a basket and a child-friendly magnetic fishing rod with its magnet securely attached.

How to Play

  1. Have the children take turns lowering the rod to catch one fish and put it in the basket.
  2. Ask each child to name their fish’s colour or compare its size with one already caught.
  3. Once everyone has had a turn, count the fish together. Hold up two and ask, “How many people do you think two fish could feed?”

Bible Lesson – Jesus Feeds the 5,000

10 min

Bible passage John 6:1–15

Before watching, ask children to listen for three numbers. How many loaves, fish and baskets do they hear about?

True or False Room Quiz

Put a TRUE ✓ sign on one side of the room and a FALSE ✗ sign on the other. Point to both signs before starting. Read one statement aloud and give the children time to walk to the side they choose. Reveal the answer once everyone has chosen, then call them back to the middle.

  1. A huge crowd followed Jesus after seeing Him heal people.
  2. Jesus asked Andrew where they could buy bread.
  3. A boy had five loaves of bread and two fish.
  4. Jesus thanked God before sharing the bread.
  5. Some people went away hungry because there wasn’t enough food.
  6. The disciples filled two baskets with leftovers.
Leader’s Answer Key
  1. True. The crowd had seen Him heal sick people.
  2. False. Jesus asked Philip. Andrew told Him about the boy’s lunch.
  3. True. He had five barley loaves and two fish.
  4. True. Jesus gave thanks, then shared the bread.
  5. False. Everyone ate as much as they wanted.
  6. False. They filled twelve baskets.

The Story in Brief

Five thousand men and many more women and children had gathered to see Jesus. A boy had just five barley loaves and two fish. Jesus asked the crowd to sit down. He gave thanks to God and shared the food. Everyone had enough to eat. Jesus asked the disciples to collect the leftovers so nothing would be wasted. They filled twelve baskets.

Jesus Connection Jesus saw that the people were hungry and cared for them. Later He said, “I am the bread that gives life.” Food keeps our bodies going each day, and Jesus gives eternal life to everyone who trusts Him.

Prayer

Dear God, thank You for the food You give us and for sending Jesus, the Bread of Life. Help us trust Him and notice when someone around us needs help. Amen.

Craft – Basket and Fish-and-Loaves Wheel

10 min

Before the Session

  • Print one basket page per child on A4 cardstock and one A4 strip sheet per three children on ordinary paper. Print both at 100% (Actual Size). Each strip is 49 mm wide and 248 mm long, including the tape tab.
  • Leaders cut along both 51 mm slit guides in the basket ahead of time. Have scissors, colouring pencils and sticky tape ready.

Steps

  1. Colour the fish and loaves. Cut one full strip along its dashed outline, including the tape tab.
  2. From behind, feed one end through the top slot. Lay the pictures face up across the basket and feed the end back through the bottom slot.
  3. On the back, overlap the ends at the tab and tape them into a loose loop. Keep the picture side facing outward and the strip flat between the slots.
  4. Slide the strip down gently to bring the next picture into the basket. Retell the miracle as the pictures go around.
