Miracles of Jesus · Week 1

Water into Wine: Jesus Shows His Glory

Ages 4–10 · 40 min
Memory Verse: “So in Cana of Galilee, Jesus did His first miracle. There He showed His glory, and His followers believed in Him.” — John 2:11 (ICB)
👋
Intro – What Is a Miracle?
5 min

Welcome the children to our new series and get them thinking about parties and big problems.

Ask the group: “What’s the best party or wedding you’ve ever been to? What made it so good?” (Let them share — affirm every response.)
Ask: “What would happen if all the food and drinks ran out in the middle of the party?” (Take a few answers.)
Leader note: Introduce the series: “For the next six weeks we’re looking at miracles — amazing things Jesus did that only God can do. Each one shows us who Jesus really is.” In Jesus’ day a wedding party could last a whole week, and running out of wine for the guests would have been very embarrassing for the family. Some children may ask about wine — keep it simple: it was the normal drink for grown-ups at a wedding back then.
🎮
Game – Fill the Jars Sponge Relay
10 min

A wet, fast relay race to fill the jars right up to the brim — just like the servants did at the wedding.

Setting Up

  • Play outside if you can. Split the children into 2–4 even teams and line each team up behind a start line.
  • Put a bucket of water and one big sponge at the start line for each team.
  • About 8–10 metres away, put a clear jar or container for each team. Mark a “brim” line near the top with tape or a marker.

How to Play

  1. On “Go!”, the first child in each team soaks the sponge in the bucket.
  2. They run to their team’s jar, squeeze every drop of water in, then run back and hand the sponge to the next person.
  3. Keep going until one team fills their jar up to the brim line — they’re the winners!
Tip: Pair younger children with an older buddy so they don’t get left behind. Use plastic containers, not glass, and have a towel or two handy for slippery hands and feet.
Leader note: When the game is over, ask: “Was that hard work?” At the wedding, Jesus told the servants to fill six big stone jars with water, and each one held about 75–115 litres! They filled them right to the brim. It was a strange thing to ask, but they did what Jesus said — and watch what happened next.

The Story in Brief

Jesus, His mother Mary and His disciples were invited to a wedding in a town called Cana. In the middle of the party, the wine ran out! Mary came to Jesus and told Him about the problem. Then she said to the servants, “Do whatever He tells you.” Nearby stood six big stone water jars. Jesus told the servants, “Fill the jars with water,” and they filled them right up to the brim. Then He said, “Now take some out and give it to the man in charge of the party.” When the man tasted it, the water had become wine — and not just any wine, the very best! He said to the bridegroom, “You have saved the best wine until now!” This was the first miracle Jesus did. It showed His glory, and His disciples believed in Him.

Jesus Connection: Only God can turn plain water into wine with just a word. This miracle was a sign that showed us who Jesus is — God’s own Son, with power over everything He made. When things went wrong at the party, Jesus didn’t just fix the problem, He gave them something even better. That’s what Jesus came to do for us. Our biggest problem is sin, and we can’t fix it ourselves. But Jesus died on the cross and rose again to take our sin away and give us something far better — new life with God forever. His disciples saw His glory and believed in Him, and we can trust Him too.

🙏 Prayer

Father, thank You for Jesus, who can do what no one else can do. Thank You that He turned water into the very best wine and showed us His glory. Thank You that He came to give us something even better — forgiveness and new life with You. Help us to believe in Him and to do whatever He tells us, just like the servants did. Amen.

Craft – Water-to-Wine Slider Jar
10 min

Children make a stone water jar with a sliding window. Push the strip down and the jar is full of water. Pull it up and the water has become wine — just like Jesus did at the wedding.

Music: Play Who Is This? in the background while the children make their craft, so they start to learn the song.

Before the Session

  • Print one template page per child on card (200 gsm works well). Use the colour page, or the black-and-white page for children to colour.
  • For younger children: cut out the jars, window and slider strip ahead of time, so they can go straight to gluing and sliding.
  • The window is the trickiest part to cut. A leader can pre-cut it with a craft knife.

Materials

  • Printed template — front jar, back jar and slider strip (one set per child)
  • Scissors (or pre-cut pieces for younger children)
  • Glue stick
  • Crayons or markers (only if using the black-and-white page)

Steps

  1. Colour the jar, water and wine (black-and-white page only).
  2. Cut out the front jar and its window, the back jar, and the slider strip.
  3. Put glue on the two striped sides of the back jar only — keep the middle and top free of glue.
  4. Press the front jar on top so the edges line up, and let it stick.
  5. Hold the strip with PULL UP at the top and slide it into the jar mouth, water end first, until WATER shows in the window.
  6. Pull the tab up — the water has become THE BEST WINE!
Leader note: As the children slide their jars, ask: “Could you or I turn water into wine?” (No!) “Who could?” (Only Jesus!) Each time they pull the tab, say together: “Jesus can do what no one else can!”
